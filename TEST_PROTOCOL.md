# SpendSense AI: Elite Edition Test Protocol 🛡️

Follow these steps exactly to verify that your financial intelligence engine is 100% robust and "Elite."

---

## Phase 1: Mission Setup (Foundation)
1.  **Total Reset**: Go to **Settings**. Click **"Clear All Data"** to ensure you're testing from zero.
2.  **The Hard Cap**: In **Settings**, set a **Monthly Spending Cap** of **₹50,000**.
3.  **Currency**: Select **INR (₹)** as your primary currency.
4.  **Verification**: Go to the **Dashboard**. Verify the **"Pilot Setup Required"** card is gone and replaced by the **Burn Rate Monitor**.

---

## Phase 2: Data Infiltration (Ingestion)
1.  **Smart Upload**: Go to **Upload**. Drag and drop a messy CSV or Excel file.
2.  **Parsing Check**: Verify that the **Smart Parser** automatically cleaned the merchant names (e.g., lowercase to Title Case) and dates.
3.  **Context Sync**: Go to **Transactions**. Verify that the uploaded data is visible and correctly categorized.

---

## Phase 3: Ledger God-Mode (CRUD & Portability)
1.  **Inline Edit**: Click the **✏️ Edit** icon on any transaction. Change the amount or merchant and click **Save**. Verify it updates instantly.
2.  **The Purge**: Click the **🗑️ Delete** icon. Confirm the deletion and verify the **Burn Rate** on the dashboard updates accordingly.
3.  **Elite Export**: Click **📥 Export Ledger**. Open the downloaded file and verify it’s a perfectly formatted clean CSV.

---

## Phase 4: Financial Vitals (Math Engines)
1.  **Velocity Check**: On the **Dashboard**, verify the **"Daily Velocity"** (e.g., ₹1,500/day) is accurately calculated based on current month spend.
2.  **The Rollover**: If you have transactions from *last month* (e.g., March 2024), verify that the unspent balance from your ₹50,000 cap appears as a pulsing **"Rollover Bonus"** badge in the header.

---

## Phase 5: The Ghost Hunt (Subscriptions)
1.  **Pattern Recognition**: Go to **AI Insights**.
2.  **Audit Check**: Verify the **"Ghost Hunter: Subscription Audit"** section. It should list any merchant that appeared at least twice in a 30-day interval (e.g., Netflix or Gym).
3.  **Commitment**: Check the **"Monthly Commitment"** total. It should be the sum of all detected recurring costs.

---

## Phase 6: Elite AI Coaching (Chat Intelligence)
1.  **Context Test**: Go to **AI Chat**. Ask: *"What is my current monthly cap and am I overspending?"*
2.  **Analysis**: The AI should correctly state your **₹50,000 cap** and analyze your current spend totals.
3.  **Visual Markers**: Ask: *"Analyze my financial health."*
4.  **UI Verification**: Verify the AI renders a stylized **Health Progress Bar** or a **Status Badge (OK/CRITICAL)** directly inside its chat bubble.

---

## Phase 7: Aesthetic Audit (Elite Polish)
1.  **Stealth Scrollbar**: Scroll down the Transactions list. Verify the scrollbar is thin, dark-themed, and uses the **Primary Blue** color.
2.  **Alignment Fix**: Check the **Navbar** (Top Right). Verify that **"Raghav Premium"** is perfectly visible and has a generous margin from the edge of the screen.
3.  **Glassmorphism**: Hover over the Sidebar and Dashboard cards. Verify the subtle glass-blur and border-glow effects.

---

### 🏆 SUCCESS CRITERIA
If all 7 phases pass, **SpendSense AI** is officially bulletproof.
